## Generation prompt

Create a premium editorial lifestyle photograph for a playful personality-first BLIND DATING app, portrait 4:5 composition. A real candid first-date moment at a beautiful outdoor neighborhood bistro at golden hour: close-up of TWO adults' hands meeting across a small round cherry-red lacquer café table, gently clinking two short clear glasses of sparkling citrus soda with orange slices. Only their hands and cropped arms/sleeves visible: one cream linen shirt, one soft lilac knit sleeve, two subtly different skin tones. NO faces, NO heads, NO recognizable identities, no legs. A small red-orange gerbera in a lilac ceramic bud vase, a half-open menu with NO readable lettering, warm dappled afternoon sunlight and a little direct-flash editorial texture, soft pale yellow-green street bokeh. Modern fashion campaign quality, intimate but easygoing, believable human hands with accurate anatomy and realistic glass reflections. Warm cream, soft lilac, rich cherry red, peach colors, natural film grain and deep warm shadows. Camera slightly elevated three-quarter closeup looking toward the table, candid asymmetrical framing, large table surface and hands dominate, no dark vignette. The image should feel flirty, tactile, optimistic, spontaneous and distinctly about a first date, not wedding or stock corporate lifestyle. NO typography, NO logos, NO UI, NO heart graphics, NO borders. This is atmospheric brand photography, not a photograph of any sample match.
